What's happening

Why do feelings get so intense during puberty?

Emotions get intense during puberty due to lots of hormones changing in your body and brain development.

These hormones happen because the brain is preparing itself to deal with stronger and more complex emotions.

Hormones aren't the only factor. Peer pressure, lack of sleep, school demands and more also play a role in your emotions and mood swings.

You don't have to do this alone

Talking about it is always an option

Your never alone in this and can always talk to someone you trust.

Talking about it can help you feel better and find solutions. It also lets other people know what you're going through.

Talk to a trusted friend

Try starting with: "I've been feeling really overwhelmed lately and I could use someone to talk to. Can we chat?"

Call a helpline

Try starting with: "Hi, I'm a teen and I'm going through some stuff emotionally. I'm not sure where to start, can you help?"

Talk to a school counselor

Try starting with: "I've been dealing with some big feelings and I'd like to talk to someone about it. Could we set up a time to chat?"
